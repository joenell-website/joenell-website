Engagement photos are the warm-up for your wedding photos, and often the first photos of the two of you that you actually love. Vancouver makes it easy: ocean, mountains, forest and gardens are all within a short drive.

I'm Joenell, a Vancouver engagement photographer and wedding photographer. This guide covers everything I share with couples before a session: where to go, when the light is best, what to wear, how the session works and what it costs.

Why Book an Engagement Session?

  • Get comfortable on camera. Most couples say they're "awkward in photos". An hour together fixes that before the wedding day.
  • Get to know your photographer. You'll learn how I guide you, and I'll learn what makes you laugh.
  • Use the photos. Save-the-dates, your wedding website, a guest book or simply your walls.

The Best Engagement Photo Locations in Vancouver

Pick a place that feels like you, then think about the light and how busy it gets. These are the spots couples choose most:

Stanley Park and the seawall

Ocean sunsets, a rose garden, Lost Lagoon and forest trails in one park. See my full Stanley Park photo guide.

Beaches: Spanish Banks, Kitsilano and English Bay

Wide sand, mountain views and golden-hour sun over the water. Spanish Banks at low tide is one of the quietest and most beautiful.

Gardens: Queen Elizabeth Park and VanDusen

Flowers from spring to autumn, paths, bridges and city views. Lovely in April and May, and again in October.

City streets: Gastown and Granville Island

Brick, cobblestones and string lights for a city feel. Best early in the morning or on a weekday, before the crowds.

North Shore: Lighthouse Park and Whytecliff Park

Moss, tall trees, rocky shores and ocean views. The forest light is soft even on cloudy days.

The Best Time for Engagement Photos

Time of day matters more than anything else. I plan most sessions for the hour or two before sunset, when the light is soft, warm and flattering.

  • Spring (March to May): cherry blossoms, tulips and rhododendrons.
  • Summer: long evenings; sunset is around 9:20 pm in late June.
  • Autumn: golden leaves in October, and softer, earlier sunsets.
  • Winter: sunset is around 4:15 pm in December, so plan an early-afternoon session; snowy mountains make a great backdrop.

Sunrise sessions are wonderful too: calm water, soft light and empty beaches. Read more in the best time of year for engagement photos in Vancouver.

What to Wear

  • Coordinate, don't match. Pick two or three soft colours that work together.
  • Avoid busy patterns and logos. They pull attention from your faces.
  • Add texture. Knits, linen and flowing fabrics look great in motion.
  • Dress for the place. Flowing dresses for beaches and gardens; layers and boots for the North Shore.
  • Comfortable shoes for walking between spots, then change for photos.

How a Session Works

  1. Plan together. We choose the place, the time and a backup in case of rain.
  2. Warm up. We start with walking and talking, so the first photos are relaxed, not posed.
  3. Easy prompts. Simple directions like "walk towards me and whisper something" lead to real laughs.
  4. Golden hour. We finish in the best light of the day.
  5. Your gallery. A sneak peek within 48 hours, then at least 30 edited images within 2 weeks.

How to Prepare: a Quick Checklist

  • Steam your outfits the night before.
  • Clean your rings: they'll be in close-ups.
  • Bring water, a hair tie, lip balm and a jacket.
  • Arrive 10 minutes early so you're not rushed.
  • Plan a nice dinner afterwards: it makes the session feel like a date.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do engagement photos cost in Vancouver?

My engagement sessions are $225 for up to 60 minutes and at least 30 professionally edited images. A 20% non-refundable deposit reserves your date, and the balance is due 1 week before the session.

How long is an engagement session?

Up to 60 minutes. That's enough for two or three spots close together, with time to relax into it.

When will we get our engagement photos?

You'll get a sneak peek within 48 hours and the full gallery within 2 weeks.

How far ahead should we book?

For spring blossoms, summer evenings and autumn colour, book a few weeks ahead. Weekday evenings are easier to get than weekends.

What if it rains?

We can move the date, or lean into it: covered spots, forest trails and a clear umbrella make beautiful, moody photos.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes. Let me know in advance so we pick a dog-friendly location, and bring a friend who can take your dog home after a few photos.
