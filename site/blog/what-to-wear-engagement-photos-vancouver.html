Planning your engagement session?

Here's everything you need to know about choosing outfits that look beautiful, feel natural, and help tell your unique love story.

Your Love Story Deserves More Than Beautiful Clothes

Years from now, when you look back at your engagement photographs, you probably won't remember where your sweater came from or what shoes you were wearing.

You'll remember the laughter.

The butterflies before the session began.

The way your partner looked at you while the sun disappeared behind the mountains.

You'll remember beginning one of the most exciting chapters of your lives together.

That's exactly why choosing what to wear for your engagement session isn't about following fashion trends.

It's about wearing something that allows you to feel comfortable, confident, and completely yourselves.

As a Vancouver engagement photographer, one of the first questions couples ask me is:

"What should we wear?"

"What should we wear?"

The answer is actually much simpler than most people expect.

You don't need expensive designer clothing.

You don't need perfectly matching outfits.

You don't need to look like models.

You simply need clothing that complements your personalities, your chosen location, and allows your connection to become the focus of every photograph.

This guide will walk you through everything you need to know before your engagement session—from choosing colours and fabrics to dressing for Vancouver's ever-changing weather.

Whether you're planning a sunset session at English Bay, exploring the forests of Lighthouse Park, or celebrating your engagement among the gardens of Queen Elizabeth Park, these tips will help you look and feel your very best.

Why Your Outfit Choice Matters More Than You Think

The purpose of engagement photography isn't to show off your wardrobe.

It's to celebrate your relationship.

The right outfit quietly supports that story.

The wrong outfit can unintentionally distract from it.

Simple, timeless clothing allows your expressions, your connection, and your emotions to become the centre of every image.

Years from now, timeless photographs will still feel elegant.

Trendy outfits often don't.

That's why I always encourage couples to choose clothing that reflects who they are today while avoiding styles that may quickly feel dated.

Beautiful engagement photography is never about wearing the most expensive clothes.

It's about feeling completely comfortable beside the person you love.

And when you feel comfortable...

Everything else naturally follows.

You'll laugh more.

Move naturally.

Forget the camera is even there.

Those genuine moments become the photographs you'll treasure for the rest of your lives.

Before You Choose Your Outfit, Choose Your Location

One mistake many couples make is selecting their outfits before deciding where they'll be photographed.

Instead, think about the story you want your photographs to tell.

Do you imagine yourselves walking barefoot along the beach?

Exploring forest trails together?

Watching the sunset over the ocean?

Or enjoying the elegance of Vancouver's beautiful gardens?

Your location should inspire your wardrobe—not the other way around.

In Vancouver, every location creates a different mood.

The clothing that looks perfect at English Bay may feel completely out of place in the forests of Lynn Canyon.

Planning your outfits around your surroundings creates a gallery that feels cohesive, intentional, and beautifully timeless.

Dress for the Story You Want to Tell

Every engagement session tells a different story.

Some couples want their photographs to feel adventurous.

Others imagine a quiet evening watching the sunset together.

Some love dressing up for elegant portraits, while others simply want to wear their favourite sweater and walk hand in hand through the forest.

Neither approach is right or wrong.

The most beautiful engagement photographs happen when your clothing feels like an extension of your personality.

Before opening your wardrobe, ask yourselves one simple question:

"How do we want these photographs to feel?"

Romantic?

Elegant?

Playful?

Relaxed?

Adventurous?

Your answer will naturally guide the clothing you choose.

When your outfits complement the story instead of competing with it, every photograph feels more authentic.

Choosing Colours That Photograph Beautifully

Colour has an incredible impact on the mood of your photographs.

While there are no strict rules, some colour palettes consistently create timeless images.

Soft neutrals such as cream, beige, ivory, light grey, camel, and white remain some of my favourites because they allow your connection—not your clothing—to become the centre of attention.

Earth tones such as olive green, rust, tan, soft brown, and muted blues also photograph beautifully against British Columbia's forests and mountains.

These colours work especially well throughout Vancouver because they naturally complement the landscapes rather than overpowering them.

Bright neon colours and heavily saturated tones often draw attention away from your expressions.

Likewise, clothing with very large logos, busy graphics, or bold text can quickly become the first thing people notice in a photograph.

Years from now, timeless colours will continue to look elegant.

That's one of the reasons I always encourage couples to keep things simple.

Should We Match?

This might surprise you.

No.

Matching outfits are no longer the goal.

Instead, think about coordinating.

Imagine your clothing as two pieces of the same story rather than identical copies of one another.

Some combinations that photograph beautifully include:

  • Cream and navy
  • White and olive green
  • Beige and light blue
  • Camel and charcoal
  • Sage green and cream
  • Soft blush and light grey

These combinations create harmony while still allowing each person's personality to shine.

One partner doesn't need to disappear into the other's style.

Instead, your outfits should complement one another naturally.

Dress for Vancouver's Incredible Locations

One of the biggest advantages of photographing couples in Vancouver is the incredible variety of landscapes available within a short drive.

Your clothing should enhance each environment.

Beach Sessions

Locations such as English Bay, Spanish Banks, and Whytecliff Park pair beautifully with:

  • Linen shirts
  • Flowing dresses
  • Soft neutral colours
  • Barefoot walks along the sand
  • Comfortable sandals

The goal is effortless elegance.

Ocean breezes naturally add movement to dresses and hair, creating photographs that feel relaxed and romantic.

Forest Sessions

For Lighthouse Park, Lynn Canyon, Pacific Spirit Regional Park, and similar locations, consider:

  • Earth tones
  • Cozy sweaters
  • Denim jackets
  • Boots
  • Layered clothing

These textures beautifully complement Vancouver's towering evergreens and create warmth throughout the photographs.

Garden Sessions

Planning your engagement session at Queen Elizabeth Park or VanDusen Botanical Garden?

Soft colours work wonderfully.

Think:

  • Cream
  • Sage green
  • Dusty blue
  • Soft pink
  • Light lavender
  • Beige

These colours harmonize with flowers and greenery while keeping your photographs elegant and timeless.

Mountain Sessions

Whistler, Cypress Mountain, and the Sea-to-Sky Corridor offer dramatic scenery unlike anywhere else in British Columbia.

Mountain sessions are perfect for:

  • Long coats
  • Knit sweaters
  • Scarves
  • Boots
  • Layered textures

Not only do layers keep you comfortable, but they also add depth and visual interest to your photographs.

Dress for the Season

British Columbia offers stunning engagement sessions all year long.

Each season creates its own unique atmosphere.

Spring

Cherry blossoms begin to bloom, parks become vibrant, and temperatures are comfortable.

Light layers and pastel colours complement the fresh landscape beautifully.

Summer

Summer offers long evenings, warm temperatures, and Vancouver's famous golden sunsets.

Flowing dresses, linen fabrics, and breathable clothing help couples stay comfortable while creating timeless images.

Autumn

Autumn transforms Vancouver into one of Canada's most colourful cities.

Warm earth tones such as rust, camel, olive, and burgundy perfectly complement the golden leaves.

It's one of my favourite seasons for engagement photography.

Winter

Winter sessions can feel incredibly romantic.

Long coats, scarves, textured sweaters, and boots create cozy photographs while allowing couples to embrace Vancouver's quieter parks and nearby snowy mountain scenery.

Winter engagement sessions often feel intimate, peaceful, and wonderfully cinematic.

A Style Guide for Him

Gentlemen, here's some good news.

You don't need to buy an entirely new wardrobe for your engagement session.

In fact, some of the best photographs come from clothing you already own and genuinely enjoy wearing.

The goal isn't to look like someone else.

It's to look like the best version of yourself.

Choose clothing that fits well, feels comfortable, and allows you to move naturally.

A well-fitted outfit will always photograph better than expensive clothing that feels stiff or uncomfortable.

My Favourite Choices for Men

For most Vancouver engagement sessions, I recommend:

  • Neutral knit sweaters
  • Linen or cotton button-up shirts
  • Chinos or well-fitted trousers
  • Dark jeans without excessive distressing
  • Leather boots
  • Clean white sneakers (depending on the location)
  • Casual blazers for a more refined look

Layers also photograph beautifully.

Adding a jacket, overshirt, or lightweight coat creates depth and variety throughout your gallery.

What to Avoid

Try to avoid:

  • Large logos
  • Sports jerseys
  • Neon colours
  • Cargo shorts
  • Wrinkled clothing
  • Athletic running shoes (unless the session is specifically outdoors or hiking)

Remember...

The focus should always remain on your relationship—not your outfit.

A Style Guide for Her

One question I hear all the time is:

"Should I wear a dress?"

"Should I wear a dress?"

The answer is...

Only if it feels like you.

Many women love wearing flowing dresses because they create beautiful movement in photographs, especially at beaches and gardens.

But others feel far more comfortable wearing jeans with a cozy sweater.

Both can look absolutely stunning.

Confidence always photographs better than fashion.

Choose something that allows you to relax, laugh, walk comfortably, and simply enjoy spending time together.

Dresses That Photograph Beautifully

If you enjoy dresses, consider:

  • Maxi dresses
  • Midi dresses
  • Soft flowing fabrics
  • Neutral colours
  • Earth tones
  • Pastel shades

Movement creates life in photographs.

A gentle breeze at English Bay or Whytecliff Park can transform a simple dress into something truly magical.

Don't Forget Comfort

Many engagement sessions involve walking.

Sometimes we'll explore beaches.

Sometimes forest trails.

Sometimes gardens.

Choose shoes you can comfortably walk in.

If you're planning to wear heels, consider bringing comfortable shoes for walking between locations.

Your feet will thank you.

Accessories That Add Personality

Accessories should complement your outfit—not overwhelm it.

Some wonderful additions include:

  • Wide-brim hats
  • Scarves during autumn and winter
  • Elegant watches
  • Delicate necklaces
  • Simple earrings
  • Fresh flowers
  • A cozy blanket for sitting together
  • Champagne for celebrating afterwards
  • A handwritten letter to each other
  • Your favourite coffee from the café where you had your first date

Small personal details often become some of the most meaningful parts of your photographs.

They tell your story.

Many couples choose:

Hair and Makeup Tips

Professional hair and makeup isn't required.

But if you're considering it, engagement sessions are actually a wonderful opportunity.

Not only will you feel confident during your photographs, but you'll also have the chance to see how your makeup photographs before your wedding day.

Many brides use their engagement session as a trial run with their wedding hair and makeup artist.

It's a practical way to make adjustments long before the wedding arrives.

For both partners, avoid trying anything completely new just before your session.

Fresh haircuts, unfamiliar makeup products, or dramatic style changes are often best tested ahead of time rather than the day before your photographs.

The goal is simple:

Look like yourself.

Just a little more polished.

Common Outfit Mistakes Couples Make

Even the most beautiful location can't completely hide an outfit that feels distracting or uncomfortable. Fortunately, these mistakes are easy to avoid with a little planning.

Wearing identical outfits

Instead of matching perfectly, choose colours that complement one another. Your outfits should feel connected, not identical.

Choosing uncomfortable shoes

Many Vancouver engagement sessions involve walking through parks, beaches, gardens, or forest trails. Comfortable footwear allows you to enjoy the experience and move naturally.

Wearing clothing with large logos or graphics

Logos, slogans, and bold graphics often become the first thing people notice in a photograph. Timeless clothing keeps the attention where it belongs—on your connection.

Trying a completely new look

Whether it's a brand-new haircut, unfamiliar makeup, or shoes you've never worn before, your engagement session isn't the ideal time for major style experiments.

Forgetting to steam your clothing

Wrinkles are surprisingly noticeable in photographs. Taking a few minutes to prepare your outfits beforehand makes a significant difference.

The Little Things Couples Often Forget

After photographing couples throughout British Columbia, I've noticed a few small details that are easy to overlook.

Consider bringing:

  • Water
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Lip balm
  • Hair ties
  • A small towel during summer
  • Touch-up makeup
  • A jacket for cooler evenings
  • Snacks if we're exploring multiple locations

These little comforts help you stay relaxed, especially during longer sessions.

And when you're relaxed...

That's when the magic happens.

Your Engagement Session Is About More Than Beautiful Photos

One of the biggest misconceptions about engagement photography is that it's simply another photoshoot.

It isn't.

It's often the first opportunity you'll have to slow down together after the excitement of getting engaged.

Between planning your wedding, telling family and friends the good news, choosing a venue, and thinking about everything still to come, life becomes busy very quickly.

Your engagement session gives you permission to pause.

To laugh.

To hold hands.

To enjoy each other's company without worrying about seating charts, timelines, or guest lists.

Some couples tell me afterwards that it felt more like a date than a photoshoot.

That's exactly how it should feel.

Bring Your Personality With You

The most memorable engagement sessions aren't defined by the location.

They're defined by the people in them.

Think about the little things that make your relationship unique.

Perhaps you both love coffee and want to visit your favourite café.

Maybe you enjoy hiking together.

Perhaps your dog has been part of your journey from the very beginning.

Maybe Friday evenings always end with pizza and a walk along the beach.

Those small details matter.

Your engagement session should feel like your relationship—not someone else's Pinterest board.

The more personal we make the experience, the more meaningful your photographs become.

Can We Bring Our Dog?

Absolutely.

They're family.

Some of my favourite engagement sessions have included four-legged companions excitedly running along the beach, exploring forest trails, or sitting proudly beside their humans.

If you'd like your dog to join us, I recommend bringing:

  • Water
  • Treats
  • A favourite toy
  • Waste bags
  • A leash
  • A trusted friend or family member who can take your dog home once we've finished photographing them

This allows us to include your furry family member while still giving you time for romantic portraits together afterwards.

Frequently Asked Questions About Engagement Session Outfits

Planning your engagement session often comes with plenty of questions. Here are some of the ones couples ask me most often.

How Many Outfits Should We Bring?

For most engagement sessions, I recommend bringing one or two outfits.

One outfit is perfectly fine if you prefer to keep things simple and relaxed.

If your session allows enough time, a second outfit can add wonderful variety to your gallery.

Many couples choose:

Outfit One Comfortable and casual.

Perfect for walking, laughing, and exploring together.

Outfit Two A little more refined and elegant.

Ideal for golden-hour portraits or a more sophisticated look.

Changing outfits creates the feeling of two completely different sessions without needing to visit multiple locations.

Should We Wear Matching Colours?

Not exactly.

Instead of matching perfectly, aim to coordinate.

Choose colours that complement each other while allowing each person's style to shine.

Think harmony rather than uniformity.

The result feels more natural and timeless.

What If It Rains?

This is Vancouver.

Rain is simply part of living on the West Coast.

If the forecast calls for heavy rain, we'll discuss the best option together.

Sometimes that means adjusting the time.

Sometimes it means rescheduling.

And sometimes...

It means embracing the weather.

Soft rain, dramatic clouds, reflections on the pavement, and cozy umbrellas can create incredibly romantic photographs that feel uniquely Vancouver.

Can We Include Multiple Locations?

Absolutely.

Many couples choose two nearby locations to create more variety throughout their gallery.

For example:

  • Lighthouse Park and Whytecliff Park
  • Queen Elizabeth Park and False Creek
  • English Bay and Stanley Park

Choosing locations that are close together allows us to maximize photography time while minimizing travel.

What If We Feel Nervous?

You probably will.

Almost everyone does.

And that's completely normal.

The good news is that nervousness usually disappears within the first few minutes.

We'll walk.

Talk.

Laugh.

I'll gently guide you throughout the session without making anything feel forced.

Before long, you'll stop thinking about posing and simply enjoy spending time together.

That's when the most meaningful photographs happen.

Why Engagement Sessions Matter

Some couples wonder whether engagement photographs are really necessary.

The answer depends on what they mean to you.

For many, they're the first professional photographs they've ever had together.

For others, they're used for wedding websites, save-the-date cards, invitations, or framed prints in their home.

But perhaps their greatest value is much simpler.

They preserve a chapter of your story that you'll never experience again.

The excitement.

The anticipation.

The quiet moments before becoming husband and wife.

Life moves quickly.

Your engagement lasts only a short season.

Your photographs allow you to revisit that season for decades to come.
