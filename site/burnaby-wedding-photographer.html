Questions couples ask

Do you photograph weddings in Burnaby?

Yes. I'm based in Vancouver, minutes from Burnaby, and photograph weddings, elopements, engagement sessions and surprise proposals across the city. Burnaby is part of Metro Vancouver, so there's no extra travel charge.

Where are good photo spots in Burnaby?

Deer Lake Park (the lakeside boardwalk and gardens), Burnaby Mountain Park with its rose garden and views over Burrard Inlet, Central Park's tall forest trails, and the waterfront at Barnet Marine Park are all lovely. Some locations need a permit — I'll help you check.

When is the best time for photos in Burnaby?

Late spring brings blossoms and rhododendrons, summer gives long golden evenings, and autumn turns the parks red and gold. For portraits I plan around the hour before sunset, whatever the season.

Can we elope in Burnaby?

Absolutely. The Intimate Elopement ($850, up to 90 minutes) suits a quiet lakeside or garden ceremony; the Adventure Elopement ($1,250, up to 3 hours and 2 locations) leaves time for Burnaby Mountain at sunset.
