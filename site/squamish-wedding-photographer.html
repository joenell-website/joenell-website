Squamish Weddings

Squamish weddings and elopements, unhurried and real.

Squamish sits where Howe Sound meets the mountains, so one day can hold ocean views, granite cliffs, waterfalls and old forest. It's one of the easiest places near Vancouver for an adventurous elopement or a relaxed outdoor wedding.

Questions couples ask

Do you photograph weddings in Squamish?

Yes — weddings, elopements, engagement sessions and surprise proposals in Squamish and along the Sea to Sky. I'm about an hour away in Vancouver.

Where can we elope in Squamish?

Popular choices include lookouts over Howe Sound, forest trails and waterfalls near town. Some provincial parks require permits, so we'll check together before you book.

What's the best time of day for photos?

The hour or two before sunset gives soft, warm light on the water and mountains. Early mornings are quieter if you'd like popular spots to yourselves.

Yes. Engagement sessions start at $225 and surprise proposals at $250.
