Questions couples ask

Do you travel to Whistler?

Yes. I'm based in Vancouver and photograph weddings, elopements and engagement sessions in Whistler and along the Sea to Sky. Travel is quoted with your collection.

When is the best time of year for a Whistler wedding?

Summer and early autumn give the most reliable weather and long evenings; winter weddings offer snow and cosy indoor light. We plan around whichever season you choose.

Where are good portrait spots in Whistler?

Lost Lake, Green Lake and Alta Lake are favourites for water and mountain views, and the forest trails near the village are lovely in soft light. Some spots require permits — I'll help you check.

Can we elope in Whistler?

Absolutely. Yes. The Intimate Elopement ($850, up to 90 minutes) suits a lakeside ceremony; the Adventure Elopement ($1,250, up to 3 hours and 2 locations) leaves time for a hike or a second spot.
