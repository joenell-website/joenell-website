Questions couples ask

Do we need a permit?

Some parks and gardens require a permit for ceremonies or commercial photography. I'll help you check what applies to your spot.

Can family come?

Absolutely. Elopements can be just the two of you or include a small group.

What about the marriage licence?

You'll need a BC marriage licence and a licensed officiant — we can plan the timing around them.

What if the weather turns?

We'll pick a backup and keep the plan flexible — mountain weather changes fast.
