Good to know

How many photos will we receive?

Every collection promises a minimum number of professionally edited images: The Vow 75, The Story 160, The Complete Story 250, Intimate Elopement 50, Adventure Elopement 100, surprise proposals 20 and engagement sessions 30. These are minimums: most couples receive more. Every photo is hand-edited, and I deliver every meaningful moment, never near-identical duplicates.

When will we get our photos?

You'll get a sneak peek first — within 24 hours for proposals, 48 hours for engagement sessions and 72 hours for weddings and elopements. Full galleries arrive within 1 week (proposals), 2 weeks (engagements), 3–4 weeks (elopements) and 4, 6 or 8 weeks for The Vow, The Story and The Complete Story. June–September weddings may take up to 2 extra weeks, and rush delivery in half the time is available for +$150.

Are prices in Canadian dollars?

Yes, all prices are in CAD.

Can I customize a collection?

Yes — coverage, locations and extras can all be adjusted. Send me your date and plans and I'll reply with options.

How do I hold my date?

Start with the Book your date form. Once we've confirmed the details, I'll send what's needed to reserve your date.

Do you travel?

Yes — across Greater Vancouver, the North Shore, Whistler and the rest of BC. Ask about travel for locations further afield.
