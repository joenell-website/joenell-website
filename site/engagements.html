Questions couples ask

What should we wear?

Comfortable outfits in soft, complementary colours work beautifully. See my guide to what to wear for engagement photos in Vancouver.

When is the best time for photos?

The hour or two before sunset gives the softest light. Spring blossoms and autumn colour are especially lovely in Vancouver.

How do surprise proposals work?

We agree on a spot and a signal. I stay hidden until you propose, then capture the moment and a few portraits afterwards.

Can we bring our dog?

Of course — just let me know in advance so we can plan the location.
