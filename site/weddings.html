Questions couples ask

How far ahead should we book?

Popular dates — especially summer and early autumn Saturdays — book up early, so it's best to reach out as soon as you have a date and venue.

Do you travel outside Vancouver?

Yes. I photograph weddings across Greater Vancouver, White Rock, the North Shore, Whistler and elsewhere in BC.

Can we book a smaller collection?

Yes — The Vow Collection covers just the ceremony and portraits, and every collection can be adjusted.

What if it rains?

Vancouver weather is part of the story. We'll plan a covered backup spot, and rain often makes for beautiful, moody portraits.
