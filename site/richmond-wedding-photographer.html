Richmond Weddings

Richmond weddings, photographed calmly and honestly.

Richmond sits right on the water, so the sky is big and the evening light is long. Steveston's fishing village gives you weathered wood, boats and harbour views; Garry Point opens out to the ocean for some of the best sunsets in Metro Vancouver; and in spring the cherry blossoms turn the whole city pink.

I plan your timeline so the ceremony, family photos and portraits each land in the best light of the day — often a short walk along the dyke at sunset. Richmond is part of Metro Vancouver, so there's no extra travel charge, and we can scout a spot together beforehand. Expect gentle direction, plenty of candid moments and a gallery that feels like the day did.

Questions couples ask

Do you photograph weddings in Richmond, BC?

Yes. I photograph weddings, elopements, engagement sessions and surprise proposals all over Richmond — Steveston, City Centre, Terra Nova, Sea Island and beyond. Richmond is part of Metro Vancouver, so there's no extra travel charge.

Where are good photo spots in Richmond?

Steveston Village and Fisherman's Wharf for boats and harbour light, Garry Point Park for ocean sunsets and spring cherry blossoms, the gardens and heritage chapel at Minoru Park, Britannia Shipyards' historic buildings and the open fields of Terra Nova Rural Park. Some locations need a permit — I'll help you check.

When is the best time for photos in Richmond?

Spring is cherry blossom season, summer gives long golden evenings along the dyke, and autumn brings soft light and fewer crowds in Steveston. For portraits I plan around the hour before sunset, whatever the season.

Can we elope in Richmond?

Absolutely. The Intimate Elopement ($850, up to 90 minutes) suits a small ceremony at Minoru Chapel or in a garden; the Adventure Elopement ($1,250, up to 3 hours and 2 locations) leaves time to finish at Garry Point for sunset.
