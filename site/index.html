Where I photograph

Based in Vancouver, I photograph weddings, elopements, engagements and surprise proposals across the Lower Mainland and the Sea to Sky — from Stanley Park, Queen Elizabeth Park and VanDusen Botanical Garden to English Bay, Spanish Banks, Lighthouse Park, Cypress Mountain's Bowen Lookout, Deep Cove, Squamish and Whistler.

My approach

Your wedding day is the beginning of a new chapter.

I capture genuine moments, heartfelt emotions and timeless photographs that let you relive your story for years to come.
